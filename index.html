About SmartPark

A smarter approach to public parking management

Our Project

SmartPark is a Smart Public Parking System designed to simplify vehicle parking by displaying parking availability and allowing users to reserve parking spaces conveniently.

Future Scope

The system can be extended using IoT technology, Arduino or ESP32 controllers and sensors to detect vehicle presence and update parking availability automatically.
